# Amiga sound effects

How world 1's sound effects play on Paula: the decisions, the source rules
the port keeps, the conversion, the mixer, the scene banks and the checks.
W2 built it (2026-11-09 to 2026-11-22); music is still deferred (WM in
[open work](open-work.md)).

Scope set by the user on 2026-11-09: world 1's **sound effects** on Paula.
**Music is deferred** (see WM): every song is Ogg Vorbis and will have to be
recomposed for a tracker later. Sounds that are needlessly long may be left
out. Quality is an experiment, with Chip RAM kept in check. User decisions
(2026-11-09):
- **Chip:** spend it generously and get in as many sounds as fit, instead of
  setting a small budget.
- **No streaming:** nothing streams from Fast RAM. A long sound is cut to fit
  Chip, or dropped.
- **Panning:** four hard-panned voices, as Amiga players expect. Some of them
  will later go to music.

**Inventory (W2.1):** [sound inventory](sound-inventory.md), generated by
`tools/sound-inventory.py` (`--check` verifies it), lists every sound world 1
reaches, with its size at 8,363 Hz after the -30 dB crop, scenes, callers
and a verdict. The tool takes a closure from the world-1 rooms over object
events, scripts and creations, keeping only the builder symbols in the
area-1 patterns, and names every cut with its reason.

- **Original playback rules:**
  - GameMaker runs 32 channels (`audio_channel_num(32)`).
  - `soundPlay` carries a priority and a cut-previous flag.
  - `soundPlayOL` adds a category (footsteps, gunshot, gunwall, merchant,
    enemymove, music, waterEnemy, waterThings, UI, boss) and cuts the
    category's previous sound. Its `enemymove` and `waterEnemy` categories
    skip sounds from offscreen callers.
  - Gain is the asset volume × `global.gameVolume` × `audio_master_gain`.
  - Pitch changes: low ammo +0.8, the death and results progress bars, and
    `objPlayer_n` Alarm 8.
- **Audio state can gate gameplay RNG:**
  - `soundLand` draws its sample (`irandom_range`) only while
    `sfxGunRecharge` 27 is not playing.
  - `goalTimeField`'s Collision and the clear screen's `Result` Step draw a
    wind (`choose` of seven) only while `global.voidWind` is not playing.
  - The port must therefore know when the original sample would still be
    playing, as its source duration in 60 Hz ticks, independent of any
    Amiga cut.
  - The other audio reads (`bgmDuck`, `gunshotStop`, `levelBeginCue`,
    `sfxGemBoostLow`) only affect audio.
- **RNG audit:** the compact `GameplayWorld` (the Amiga's shipping path)
  consumes every world-1 sound draw (the inventory's table) except the
  Exploding Knees and decoy-balloon ones, which belong to W1.9.
- **What exists today:**
  - `SoundPlayScript`, `TimeFieldWind`, `BulletWallSound` and the
    `RunSession` hooks drive the registered owners.
  - The compact world, shops, run session, pause menu, clear screen,
    results and front end write `SoundRequests` (W2.3) at every world-1
    call site except the W1.9 upgrades' (see W1.9). W2.6 added the laser's
    wall hit 15, bullets and casings stopping on the shop table (metal
    32/33) and the keeper's invisible wall (rock 34/35, also its footsteps
    and landings), the frog-against-frog bump (`enmFrog` Collision with
    `enmFrog`, after the enemy Steps), and the garden's footsteps and nap-wake
    landing. The floor material for footsteps and landings is approximated
    from three probes under the player; the garden's sample choice uses its
    own stream because the front end has no game RNG.
  - **Conversion (W2.2):** every class at 8,363 Hz with a -24 dB crop; the
    seven winds as 1.5 s swells, `sfxFalling` a 1 s loop, the jetpack a
    0.5 s loop and the title sting 2.5 s. The bank comes from
    `tools/sfx-bank.py --bank` (`make amiga-sfx-bank`) with
    `tools/sfx-bank-policy.json`; `--preview` rebuilds the listening page.
    Paula's minimum period (124) bounds pitch × rate: at 8,363 Hz the guns'
    1.8 and the progress-bar fill's 2.2 fit.
  - **Routes:** `make test-sound-routes` checks the menus driver and eight
    slice routes against `tests/data/sound-routes`: world one (variant 119),
    the hard-mode death in 1-2, and six coverage variants
    (`SOUND_COVERAGE_ROUTES`; `-tour` adds `DOWNWELL_SLICE_SHOP_TOUR`, a
    climb onto the shop's cases after the visit). Together with the native
    menus route (the pause menu's six) they play all 158 kept sounds but the
    eight W1.9 ones.
  - **The Paula mixer (W2.4)** plays every sound in the normal build (W2.6):
    - `PaulaMixer.h` holds the portable plan and sequencer, and
      `AmigaPaulaAudio` is the hardware driver. It plays the DWSX pack that
      `tools/pack-amiga-sfx.py` makes from the W2.2 bank.
    - `make test-paula-mixer` runs the unit checks and the route replays.
    - `make verify-amiga-paula-mixer` plays every sound and the mixer
      exercises in FS-UAE and compares the recording with the host render.
      It runs in real time, so it needs a quiet host: under load the SDL
      disk capture drops chunks.
    - **Wiring (W2.6):** at the top of every main-loop iteration (after the
      previous one's bank changes) and before a results restart,
      `DownwellAmiga.cpp` drains the front end's ring, then the run's, into
      `AmigaSoundBanks::audio()` and calls `frame()`; a silent mixer returns
      at once. The pause menu's volume option (`masterGain`) is the mixer's
      master gain. It defaults to 1.0 rather than the original's 0.6, so a
      full-scale sound plays at Paula volume 64. The scene collision candidate build's
      goal-field winds go through the run's ring (`AmigaRunSoundAudio`);
      `AmigaWindAudio` remains only in its probe
      (`verify-amiga-wind-audio`).
    - **Native checks:** `tools/verify-world-one-native.py` and
      `tools/verify-menus-native.py` read the mixer's voice table at every
      input sample and require that nothing is missing from the loaded
      banks, that no pack fails and that every sound the host route plays
      starts natively. The host route logs carry the bank set
      (`SoundRequestLog::banks`), and `make test-paula-mixer` replays them
      through those packs.
  - **Scene banks (W2.5):** `AmigaSoundBanks` owns the mixer and four Chip
    packs from `tools/pack-amiga-sfx.py`, each sound in exactly one except
    that front-end sounds the run also plays are in both run and front:
    - `Downwell.sndrun` (410,262 bytes): gameplay, shops and pause, plus
      what they share with clear and results. Resident (slot 0) from
      gameplay entry, after the cavern atlas, until the run leaves for the
      front end, so across Retry, pause, shops, clear and results.
    - `Downwell.sndfront` (62,092): the front end, only while no run bank is
      resident.
    - `Downwell.sndresults` (18,828) and `Downwell.sndclear` (7,108): loaded
      over the run bank (slot 1) while those screens show; the clear bank is
      freed before the next level reloads the atlas.

    A pack loads only if the largest Chip block keeps 16 KiB beyond it, and
    a failed load leaves that bank silent (`gDownwellSoundBankFailures`).
    Measured at 1 MiB in W2.5: 600,104 bytes free in the front end, 45,992
    (largest block 45,944) in gameplay, and 27,160 on the results screen.
    W2.6 since shrank the run pack by 23,964 bytes (dropping the unreachable
    sounds, mostly the void ambience loop) and grew the front pack by 15,264
    (the garden's footsteps and landings and `sfxDissipate` 187).
    `chip_memory_watch.gdb` prints `SOUND_BANKS` at every file load, and
    `verify_scenes.gdb` and `verify_clear.gdb` assert the bank sequence.
- **Memory:** measured on 2026-11-20 with the normal 1 MiB build in level
  1-1 (outside a shop): 476,576 bytes of Chip are free, 476,520 of them in
  one block, so about 465 KiB. Startup leaves 651,136.

## Recording check

`make verify-amiga-paula-mixer` plays every sound and the mixer exercises in
FS-UAE and compares the real-time recording with the host render
(`tools/compare-paula-recording.py`). The capture drops 60 ms buffers, at
times one every 2-3 s even on a lightly loaded host: the comparer reports
differences next to a drop as capture loss and still fails stray sound and
anything away from a drop. When a run excuses sounds, record again until each
has matched once. On 2026-11-22 one run lost 25 buffers and excused sounds
19, 26, 34, 60, 109 and 342; the next lost 2 and matched every sound.
